01 / THE IDEA

Software can think.
Hardware makes
it useful.

Some of the most interesting engineering problems live between disciplines.

An AI agent may understand a task, but still need a reliable way to see a screen, press a key, or work with a machine that cannot run its usual tools. That gap is where our work begins.

LabXIII brings software, hardware integration, and experimental research into the same workflow: frame a problem, build a prototype, and test what it can actually do.
