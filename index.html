Commercial Buyer & Investor Brief

Why Invest. What You Are Buying.

Traditional automation breaks precisely when recovery is needed most. Semantic Remote Control (SRC) transforms bare-metal resilience into an autonomous, software-isolated physical AI appliance.

01 / THE UNADDRESSED PROBLEM

The In-Band Automation Failure Mode

Modern IT administration relies entirely on software-level management agents (SSH, RDP, Ansible, cloud APIs). When network settings break, kernel panics occur, or bootloaders corrupt, in-band access collapses instantly. Datacenters endure catastrophic downtime while waiting for physical "remote hands" technician dispatches.
